Reference tables from the game data. R&D is at the full tree unless you change it here.
How exact is this? Pilot attribute and weapon attack (FP, refits, R&D, features, suitability) match the game's battle values exactly; hit chance fits 98% of observed hit rolls. Crit chance = crit rate × (1 + features) + crit rate added after features + the crit-points term (your crit points vs the target's crit resist) − the target's crit-rate resist; this matches the in-game attack preview. Buffs gained during a fight (stacks, triggers) are not modelled unless you switch them on; use Extra bonuses for anything else.
DMG_per_shot = (srcAtk × AtrDef_coeff ÷ Nshots_base − eArmor ÷ 10) × SkillM × (1 + DMG%) × (1 + eDMGFactor%, when the target has one) × (1 + FinalDMG%), × (1 + CritDMG%) on a crit. Accessories trigger once per attack; hit and crit roll per shot.
